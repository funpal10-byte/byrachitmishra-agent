# Idea bank

Drop half-thoughts here during the week. One line is enough. Do not tidy them,
do not turn them into posts — that is the agent's job.

**Why this file exists:** every generation run currently starts from web
research, which is why some posts read like commentary on the news rather than
things you know. This is the only place your actual experience enters the
pipeline. An idea here beats anything the research step can find, because
nobody else can write it.

The generator reads this file **first**, before searching. Anything unticked
is a candidate. Tick a line when it has been used.

## Format

    - [ ] the thing you noticed, in one line
    - [ ] a claim you'd defend in a meeting but haven't written down
    - [x] used — 2026-09-14

## What belongs here

Arguments you have had more than once. Advice you gave someone this week.
A thing that went wrong and what it actually cost. A piece of received wisdom
in your field that you think is false. A question a junior asked that you had
to think about. Something you changed your mind on.

## What does not

Anything specific to a company you have worked for, with, or alongside, or to
a direct competitor of your current employer. Live campaigns, colleagues,
commercial terms, internal decisions. If writing it down feels like it needs a
disclaimer, it does not belong in this file.
