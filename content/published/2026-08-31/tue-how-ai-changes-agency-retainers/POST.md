How AI is changing marketing isn't by replacing creative work, but by making generic execution cost zero.

Historically, brand retainers were priced on deliverable volume: 15 ad variations, 4 email sequences, 20 social banners. Generative tools now produce that baseline output in seconds.

When production costs collapse, flooding channels with mid-tier assets creates noise, not market share. The constraint shifts from how fast your team can execute to how sharply your team can filter.

An algorithm can produce fifty campaign hooks, but it cannot decide which customer segment your positioning explicitly sacrifices.

AI doesn't eliminate marketers; it eliminates the leverage of charging human hours for mechanical execution.

Send this to your brand manager or agency lead before setting your next scope of work.
