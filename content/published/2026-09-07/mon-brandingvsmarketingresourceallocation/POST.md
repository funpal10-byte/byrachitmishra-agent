## Caption — copy from here

```
We confuse the difference between branding and marketing because we track them on the same spreadsheet.

Marketing is an expense designed to harvest demand that exists today. It is transactional, measurable in minutes, and its price is set by your competitors bidding against you on Google Ads.

Branding is an investment designed to lower the cost of capturing that demand tomorrow. It creates a bias in the buyer before they ever open a search tab.

When you starve branding to fund short-term performance marketing, you are not saving money. You are just borrowing demand from next quarter at a much higher interest rate.

Marketing buys sales. Branding guards margins.

Send this to the finance lead who wants to cut the brand budget to hit this month's lead target.
```
