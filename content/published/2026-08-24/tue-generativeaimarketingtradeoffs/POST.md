Most generative AI marketing output sounds identical because teams prompt for tone instead of business constraints.

Positioning requires sacrifice. If you want AI tools to output usable brand messaging, you must feed them hard operational trade-offs:

1. What product features you deliberately lack
2. Which customer segments you choose to ignore
3. Where your pricing sits relative to competitors

AI does not lack creativity. Marketing teams lack clear positioning strategy to constrain it.
