## Caption — copy from here

```
This brand case study India shows how IndiGo captured 60% market share by ignoring customer delight.

They did not win on low fares or loyalty programs. They won by cutting every amenity that delayed departure times.

By locking down a 25-minute turnaround time, IndiGo achieved 12 hours of flight time per plane daily, compared to the 10-hour industry average. Removing hot meals reduced aircraft weight and eliminated kitchen maintenance delays. They put zero positioning budget into passenger comfort and focused entirely on on-time performance metrics.

Marketing leaders often mistake delight for differentiation. Operational reliability creates a far stronger moat than subjective preference.

Forward this to a brand manager who is trying to fix positioning with discounts.
```
