> Targeting: `why this ad worked`

> Claim: Repositioning succeeds when you change the consumption occasion, not when you fight cultural resistance to a product benefit.
>
> Proof (case_study): Nestlé India's 1983 launch of Maggi noodles stalled when marketed as a convenience dinner because urban mothers experienced guilt. Nestlé repositioned the product as an after-school snack ('2-Minute Noodles'), creating a new 4 PM eating occasion.

```
Selling time-saving meals to mothers failed.

To understand why this ad worked over forty years, look at the friction Nestlé removed. When a product feature triggers buyer guilt, spending more ad money on the feature never fixes conversion.

Run this 3-step Occasion Audit before changing your messaging:
1. Identify if buyers feel guilt using your efficiency shortcut.
2. Locate an unowned time slot in your customer's daily routine.
3. Frame the product as care for someone else, not relief for the buyer.

Positioning fails when it challenges culture. It wins when it owns an occasion.
```
