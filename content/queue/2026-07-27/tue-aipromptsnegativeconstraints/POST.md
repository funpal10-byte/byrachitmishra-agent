## Caption — copy from here

```
Why most AI prompts for marketing produce generic copy, and the three negative constraints that fix it.

When you tell an LLM to sound premium, accessible, and authoritative, it averages those concepts across its training data. The result is copy that sounds like every B2B website built after 2023.

Instead of instructing tone, set structural boundaries:

1. Cap sentence length at 12 words to eliminate run-on phrasing.
2. Provide an explicit list of forbidden category buzzwords.
3. Require every claim to include a specific number or named mechanism.

Prompting is not creative writing. It is quality control.

Send this to the marketer on your team writing your AI prompt workflows.
```
