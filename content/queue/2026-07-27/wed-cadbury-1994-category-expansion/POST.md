## Caption — copy from here

```
To understand why this ad worked so well in 1994, you have to look at Cadbury's original growth ceiling.

In the early 1990s, chocolate in India was bought almost exclusively by parents for children as a reward or occasional gift. That positioning capped consumption frequency and limited the audience to a fraction of the population.

To break past that ceiling, Ogilvy shifted the target persona entirely. The 1994 'Real Taste of Life' campaign put adults front and center, showing an adult woman celebrating on a cricket pitch while eating a Dairy Milk.

It gave adults social permission to buy and eat chocolate in public without feeling foolish. The campaign unlocked double-digit annual sales growth and fundamentally redefined the category.

Real category growth rarely comes from fighting for market share inside an existing segment. It comes from granting a new segment permission to buy your product.
```
