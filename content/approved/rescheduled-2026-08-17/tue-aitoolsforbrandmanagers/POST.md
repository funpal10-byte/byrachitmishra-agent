## Caption — copy from here

```
Most AI tools for marketers get abandoned in three weeks because teams try to automate judgment, not draft creation.

When you ask an LLM to write brand positioning, it calculates the mathematical average of its training data. You get phraseology like 'customer-centric innovation' because safe averages are how the model works.

Where software actually works is pattern extraction. Feed 200 raw customer complaints into a prompt to pull out the three most common points of friction. Use it to transcribe ten sales calls and surface the exact wording customers use when asking about price.

Use AI to compress research time from ten hours to ten minutes. Never use it to decide what sacrifice your brand should make.

AI gives you faster synthesis, but strategic choice remains a human job.

Send this to a brand manager on your team who is designing their team workflow.
```
