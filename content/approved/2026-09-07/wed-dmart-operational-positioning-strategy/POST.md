Most retail analysis focuses on ad campaigns. This brand case study India shows why payment terms beat advertising.

FMCG brands usually offer higher retail margins to get shelf space. DMart flipped that leverage entirely.

By reducing payment terms from 45 days to 8, suppliers willingly traded a 2% cash discount for guaranteed working capital.

DMart does not sell shelf space; it sells inventory velocity. If an SKU does not turn over in 14 days, it is delisted.

Low price is not a messaging strategy. It is an operational balance sheet.
