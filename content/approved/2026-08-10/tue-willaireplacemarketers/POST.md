## Caption — copy from here

```
The debate around whether AI will replace marketers misses the real shift happening in agency and brand team headcount.

AI is not replacing marketers. It is collapsing the execution layer.

When a team can generate 50 ad copy variations in two minutes using an LLM, writing copy is no longer the bottleneck. The bottleneck becomes evaluating that copy against brand positioning, tone guardrails, and pricing strategy.

In most consumer brand teams, junior marketers spend up to 70% of their week on execution: drafting social copy, generating script variations, and tweaking copy formats. AI reduces that execution time significantly, but it doubles the demand for judgment.

An LLM does not know your brand's non-negotiable boundaries unless you define them. The marketers who succeed over the next three years will be those who move from asset production to strategic evaluation.

My view: Stop measuring your marketing team by how much they produce. Measure them by how sharply they define the guardrails for what AI generates.

Send this to a marketer or agency partner who needs to shift focus from asset volume to positioning strategy.
```
