## Caption — copy from here

```
The question of will AI replace marketers misses the point. It is already replacing execution, not positioning.

Generating 20 ad variants used to take a copywriter half a day. LLMs do it in four seconds. What AI cannot do is decide which variant aligns with your brand's trade-offs, or identify why your existing positioning is failing in the market.

If your job is producing assets, your margin is zero. If your job is owning positioning and trade-offs, AI is just leverage.

Send this to a colleague who is still spending hours on execution tasks that an LLM does in seconds.

#aimarketing #aiinmarketing #futureofmarketing #martech #brandstrategy
```
