## Caption — copy from here

```
In every brand case study India covers, Paper Boat gets cited for nostalgia. Their real moat was supply chain.

When Hector Beverages launched Paper Boat in 2013, traditional retail shelves were dominated by legacy beverage brands. Slotting fees in modern trade were high, and kirana stores lacked fridge space for new SKUs.

Paper Boat chose flexible pouches because flat pouches could be hung on clip strips at cash counters. This created visibility without requiring fridge space or shelf real estate. Unfilled pouches also shipped flat to the factory, cutting packaging transit volume significantly compared to glass or plastic bottles.

Great packaging design is rarely just visual storytelling. It is an operational wedge that forces a distribution advantage.
```
