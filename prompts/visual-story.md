# Editorial and visual contract

The job is to make Rachit's judgement useful to a professional peer. Begin
with a recognisable decision inside an industrial or enterprise organisation.
Show its mechanism and leave a usable action. Avoid another abstract sermon
about strategy, trade-offs or AI replacing people.

The cover must show the tension as an object someone recognises: work waiting
at an approval gate, competing requirements, or a decision checklist. Changing
the headline and adding a coloured stamp is insufficient. Supply `visual`
inside `reel_cover`, selected from `bottleneck`, `comparison`, `checklist`.
It contains 2-4 `items`, each with a short `label` (24 characters maximum) and
`detail` (48 maximum), plus `note` (60 maximum) describing its basis.
For bottleneck use exactly two items: work arriving and the blocked decision.
For comparison use exactly two items: concrete alternatives or before/after.
For checklist use 2-4 checks with the action/pass condition in each detail.
Use the note "Illustrative workflow" or "Suggested check" for constructed
examples. Never present an invented document, metric or interface as evidence.

Reel beats must carry `visual` on at least two beats after the opener, including
one checklist a viewer can use. The onscreen lines must carry the complete
argument: the automatic renderer does not speak the voiceover and cannot film
the direction field. No claim that Rachit appears on camera in alt text.
The cover and first beat are separate compositions. Subsequent beats explain
the cover's tension, rather than repeat different aphorisms on the same image.

Carousel middle slides require a `visual`. Use the same object schema.
Use short body copy alongside it: 120 characters maximum when a visual exists.
The schema supports these objects and the renderer will actually draw them.

No invented personal experiences. An empty idea bank is not permission to
invent conversations or results. Use an explicit framework or a sourced fact.
Never promise a click-through or retention improvement before it is measured.
